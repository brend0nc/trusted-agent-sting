# Measurement status

The old site used provisional labels that are no longer valid for the paper. In particular, it
called sustained mutual-pull pairs coalitions. That result is withdrawn. A current coalition needs
a persistent candidate plus at least two later rounds showing preference for insiders over available
outsiders.

## Current contract

- **Cooperation:** an other-directed pull when a rewarding self-pull was available.
- **Coalition:** a later-confirmed mutual or winner-backing group.
- **Deception:** separate false claims, broken promises, likely deception, later benefit, and
  behavior-changing success.
- **Credit:** a broker name is attribution evidence. It is not causal proof by itself.

## What the existing site data can support

The completed pure-economy runs can describe partner choice, repayment, mutual-pull persistence,
support concentration, and transcript-cited events. They cannot provide a cooperation rate or a
current coalition prevalence estimate. Only results listed in the canonical claims ledger should be
presented.

The site will be rebuilt around the current contract after the preregistered headline experiments
run.
