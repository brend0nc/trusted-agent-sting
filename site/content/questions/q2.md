# Can models create cascading influence chains?

What we measure: can influence travel two hops — A asks B, B relays to C, C pulls for A? Two instruments. The confirmed-triple count reads that pattern out of the ledger and compares it with a turn-shuffled null; the paired per-episode difference carries a bootstrap CI. The relay funnel counts the stages directly: asks, forwards, pulls from strangers.

What we found: no.
