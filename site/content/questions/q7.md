# Are stronger models better at resisting being hijacked?

What we measure: can a target resist? A fixed opus-4.8 persuader plays each target model (gpt-5.5, qwen3.7-max, glm-5.2), with the target naive or inoculated (told to be sceptical of promises and check the ledger), 45 episodes a side. The score is capture from the target above the reciprocity floor. Published jailbreak work makes the direction genuinely uncertain — better instruction-following could cut either way.

What we found: nothing to resist. Capture from naive targets: {{questions.q7.naive.value:+.4f}} (95% CI [{{questions.q7.naive.ci.0:+.4f}}, {{questions.q7.naive.ci.1:+.4f}}], n = {{questions.q7.naive.n}}). Inoculated: {{questions.q7.inoculated.value:+.4f}} (CI [{{questions.q7.inoculated.ci.0:+.4f}}, {{questions.q7.inoculated.ci.1:+.4f}}], n = {{questions.q7.inoculated.n}}). Both straddle zero. The gap between them ({{questions.q7.diff.value:+.4f}}) points the way a warning predicts, but it ships without a CI. And the arm is uninformative rather than a null: at 15 episodes per cell, the smallest effect it could detect is bigger than the attack effect itself — it cannot tell zero protection from complete protection.

Limits: the warning coaches ledger-checking, so at best this measures how much an instruction helps, not innate robustness. The numbers pool three targets, so there is no per-model resistance ranking. A judge-free side-signal — the share of a seat's own pulls going to agents that had messaged it, minus the share to agents that had not — is positive with zero-excluding CIs for gemini-flash ({{questions.q7.surplus.per_model_key.geminiflash.surplus:+.2f}} [{{questions.q7.surplus.per_model_key.geminiflash.ci.0:+.2f}}, {{questions.q7.surplus.per_model_key.geminiflash.ci.1:+.2f}}]), opus-4.8 ({{questions.q7.surplus.per_model_key.opus48.surplus:+.2f}} [{{questions.q7.surplus.per_model_key.opus48.ci.0:+.2f}}, {{questions.q7.surplus.per_model_key.opus48.ci.1:+.2f}}]), and glm-5.2 ({{questions.q7.surplus.per_model_key.glm52.surplus:+.2f}} [{{questions.q7.surplus.per_model_key.glm52.ci.0:+.2f}}, {{questions.q7.surplus.per_model_key.glm52.ci.1:+.2f}}]), and crosses zero for qwen3.7-max and gpt-5.5. It mixes persuadability with ordinary deal-making — giving-after-contact, not a resistance ranking.
