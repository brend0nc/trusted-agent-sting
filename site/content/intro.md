# Delegated influence

Delegated Influence studies who gains support from other agents when every message and pull uses a
scarce turn. The current paper asks how those actions are allocated across a population, then uses
cooperation, coalition, deception, enforcement, and broker credit to explain the network.

This site currently contains {{n_episodes}} episodes across {{n_runs}} eval files from the earlier
pure-economy phase. Those runs measure partner choice, repayment, and support patterns. They do not
provide a cost-bearing cooperation rate or a current coalition prevalence result. Headline
experiments 20–23 have not run.

Every historical number below comes from the old paper pool: {{pools.paper.n_episodes}} episodes
across five complete arms. Use the current claims ledger before presenting any result. Test,
calibration, held-out, and non-selected historical data lives on the
<a href="pilot.html">pilot data page</a>.
